# Campus Water Week

A small, replayable campus-campaign game built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The typography uses Montserrat, a geometric sans-serif inspired by charity: water’s Gotham-style branding.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. Each run lasts seven days. Choose Easy (140 points), Normal (200 points), or Hard (260 points) before your first move; reach that score to meet the campaign goal. Choose one campaign action per day, manage volunteer hours and campaign budget, and adapt to changing campus events. A high-risk action is available each day; it can deliver bigger gains or backfire and cost you campaign momentum. Its odds and possible losses are shown before you commit. Played actions leave the available choices. Score milestones trigger messages at 25, 75, 150, and 220 points. Button, milestone, and victory sounds are synthesized in-browser, so the game needs no downloaded audio files. Your best campaign score and unlocked perks are saved in your browser.

Scores represent a simulated campaign, not real-world water projects or donations. The page footer links to charity: water’s website and donation page.
