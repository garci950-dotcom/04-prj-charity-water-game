# Campus Water Week

A small, replayable campus-campaign game built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. Each run lasts seven days. Choose one campaign action per day, manage volunteer hours and campaign budget, and adapt to changing campus events. A high-risk action is available each day; it can deliver bigger gains or backfire and cost you campaign momentum. Its odds and possible losses are shown before you commit. Your best campaign score and unlocked perks are saved in your browser.

Scores represent a simulated campaign, not real-world water projects or donations.
